About Us

Company Profile

Founded in 2013 in Jinhua, Zhejiang, we operate a 6,000 m² facility with 8 assembly lines and 130 skilled workers.

Current monthly capacity is around 60,000 pcs, exported to over 40 countries including the USA, Canada, Germany, the UK, France, the Netherlands, Australia, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Japan, South Korea and Malaysia.

We understand what wholesale buyers actually care about: landed cost, container efficiency, return rate and restocking speed.

Quality Control

Raw Material Inspection

Every board batch is tested for formaldehyde emission to E1 standard; plush fabrics checked for GSM, colour fastness and REACH compliance.

In-Process Inspection

Line patrol every 2 hours, focusing on plush smoothness, sisal winding density, staple strength and platform levelness.

Final 100% + AQL Sampling

100% visual inspection of finished goods, plus AQL 2.5 sampling for load tests (25kg large / 15kg small).

Inspection & Audit Support

We welcome buyer inspections, third-party QC (SGS / BV / Intertek / TÜV) and social compliance audits.

FAQ

What is the MOQ? Can I mix models?
MOQ varies by product. Contact us for the minimum order quantity for specific items.
What exactly is the discount above 500 pcs?
500-999 pcs: 10% off. 1000+ pcs: 15% off. For larger volumes (3,000+ pcs) we negotiate a dedicated price.
Can I get a sample first? What about the sample cost?
Yes. Sample fee is 2× the factory price with freight collect; the sample fee is fully refunded against your bulk order.
Which payment methods do you accept?
T/T (30% deposit + 70% against B/L copy), PayPal (best for samples and small orders) and Western Union.
What is the lead time? Longer in peak season?
Normally 15-25 days. July-October is peak export season and may extend to 30-35 days.
Which shipping options do you offer? Can you deliver to my warehouse?
FCL sea freight is most economical (recommended above 1,000 pcs); LCL for smaller volumes; air freight or express for urgent orders. We work on FOB / CIF / DDP terms.
