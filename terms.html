1. Orders & Minimum Order Quantity

MOQ varies by product. Contact us for the minimum order quantity for specific items. Orders are governed by the Proforma Invoice (PI) issued by us and signed by both parties...

2. Prices & Discounts

Quotations are in USD on FOB Ningbo / Shanghai terms and valid for 15 days. Discounts are based on total order quantity: 10% off for 500-999 pcs and 15% off for 1,000+ pcs...

3. Payment Terms

Standard terms are a 30% deposit (T/T or PayPal) to confirm the order, with the 70% balance payable within 3 working days against a copy of the B/L...

4. Production & Lead Time

Standard lead time is 15-25 working days from receipt of deposit and approval of samples / confirmation sheet. Custom OEM packaging or structural changes require an additional 5-10 days...

5. Packing & Shipping

Standard packing is brown export cartons of 1 or 2 pcs, including an assembly manual and hardware pack, with shipping marks printed to the buyer's requirements...

6. Quality, Inspection & Claims

Before shipment we perform 100% visual inspection plus AQL 2.5 sampling, and provide inspection videos and loading photos. Buyers may inspect goods themselves or through a third party (SGS / BV / Intertek etc.) before container loading...

7. Warranty & After-Sales

Structural components (boards, posts, hardware) carry a 12-month manufacturing-defect warranty from the date of arrival. The warranty excludes normal wear such as fabric pilling or fading, sisal rope abrasion and cushion deformation...

8. Intellectual Property

For trademarks, logos, packaging designs and product drawings supplied by the buyer, the buyer warrants that it holds or has been granted the necessary rights and shall bear liability for any third-party infringement claims...

9. Privacy Policy

Information we collect: when you submit an inquiry form we collect the contact name, company name, email, WhatsApp／phone, country, destination port, item codes, quantity and requirement description that you voluntarily provide.

How we use it: solely to reply to your inquiry, provide quotations, execute orders, arrange logistics and provide after-sales support.

Retention and your rights: inquiry and order records are kept for 3 years for after-sales traceability and then deleted.

10. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These terms and any sales contract concluded under them are governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China. The parties shall first seek to resolve disputes through friendly negotiation; failing that, disputes shall be submitted to CIETAC...
